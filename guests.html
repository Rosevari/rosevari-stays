A proper home for a while.

For a few nights or a few months, in Melbourne and Brisbane.

Photo shows the feel we aim for. Not a Rosevari home.

Who stays.

Mostly people in town for work. They need a proper home for a while, not a hotel room.

Nurses.

On placements and rotations at nearby hospitals, often for weeks at a time.

Contractors.

On projects in the city, who want a real kitchen and a quiet bed.

People relocating.

Starting a new job and living somewhere settled while they find a lease.

Visitors.

Families seeing relatives, and travellers who would rather live like a local.

What a stay is like.

The better the stay, the better guests treat the home.

Looked after, start to finish.

A guide to the home.
Every home has a printed guide: how each room works, the house rules, and good places nearby.
Fast Wi-Fi.
Ready from the first night, for work calls and streaming.
Someone on call.
We check in after arrival and answer messages day and night, so small problems stay small.
Verified at booking.
Government ID is verified before a booking is confirmed. It keeps every stay safe, including yours.

House rules.

Agreed at booking, so neighbours stay happy and the home stays calm.

Up to 4 guests.

The number on the booking is the number who stay.

No parties or events.

The homes are for living in, not for gatherings.

Quiet from 10pm to 7am.

So the street and the building stay calm at night.

Melbourne and Brisbane.

Inner suburbs, close to work, transport and the good parts of town.

Melbourne.

Close to the trams, the hospitals, the universities and the city. Laneway coffee before work, and old streets to walk home through.

Brisbane.

Homes near the river, the CBD and the hospitals, for people here for work, study or family. Jacarandas in spring, and warm evenings all year.
