How we apply.

  1. We enquire.

    We ask about the listing and whether the owner might consider short stays at the advertised rent.

  2. You ask the owner.

    We send you a one-page summary and the draft consent addendum to pass on. No answer needed on the spot.

  3. We apply as normal.

    The standard application, ID and references, through your usual process and platform.

  4. Lease and addendum signed.

    The standard lease plus the signed one-page addendum. Rent starts on the 1st.

What you can show the owner.

Every promise is in writing before anything is signed.

The consent addendum.
One page covering the guest limit, house rules, insurance, cleaning, repairs, inspections and rent. Their solicitor can change it.
The insurance certificate.
A specialist short-stay policy with $20 million public liability, noting the owner's interest. Arranged before the first guest; the certificate within 7 days of signing.
