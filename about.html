About Rosevari.

A small Melbourne business, run by Jordan.

A Melbourne street.

Straight answers, in writing.

Rosevari leases homes at the advertised rent, with the owner's written consent, and runs them as short stays for people in town for work, study or family.

We are setting up our first homes now. We would rather say that plainly than oversell it. So every promise we make goes into the signed lease addendum, not just onto this website.

Jordan runs the business and is the person you deal with, from the first call to the end of the lease. We are based in Melbourne, with a local team on the ground for anything that needs someone in person.

How we work.

Consent first.

We only lease a home for short stays when the owner has agreed in writing. If the answer is no, that is the end of it.

The advertised rent.

Never more, as Victorian and Queensland tenancy law requires. We do not bid up rents to win a lease.

Everything in writing.

Insurance, cleaning, repairs, inspections and rent are all set out in a one-page addendum the owner's solicitor can change.

One person to call.

Owners, agents and guests all reach Jordan directly. No call centre, no ticket numbers.
